## Goal

Living Systems Lab is a public teaching site. A visitor explores two living grids in English or German: a food-chain meadow, and Conway's Game of Life. The Angular app is that site, not a generic starter template.

## Problem

Unconfirmed: a first encounter with these systems is usually a static explanation or a research toy. The lab exists so a visitor can change a cell and watch the local rule answer, with no account and no install.

## Audience

Visitors to the public site. They can read the lab in English or German. They do not sign in.

## Capabilities

- Meadow Makers: paint plants, plant eaters, predators, or erase; run, step, or reset; open a seeded story (balanced, green meadow, or predator boom); change speed and species behavior; read population totals and one animal's energy; see the food-chain clue. Playback stops when no animals remain.
- Conway's Way of Life: germinate, step, replant, or clear; change speed; toggle wrap-around; plant with brushes; start from a Methuselah seed; read living, sprout, perennial, and withered counts. Playback stops when nothing is alive. The rule is B3/S23, and there is no score.
- A page that explains those Conway rules and what the Life page shows.
- A language switch that survives reload.
- One legal page, linked from the footer, that states the operator and the privacy notice together. It is not a second simulation and not two routes.

## Non-goals

- Accounts, cookies, forms, and any saved visitor profile. Remembering the language choice is not a profile.

Anything not listed here is open, not forbidden.

## Principles

- Teaching happens on a grid the visitor can edit, not behind an account.
- Meadow rules and Life rules stay separate. They do not share an edge rule or a tick.
